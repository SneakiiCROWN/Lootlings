**Version:** 1.0  
**Baseline:** `fba01606974fcb6ce4f623ffef0447b377809b23`

> This file is a pre-production source of truth for art, writing, content planning, and future Codex implementation. It intentionally goes beyond what is currently coded.

## 1. Product North Star

- Cute first, badass second: tiny mythical relic-beasts carrying absurdly oversized equipment.
- Life in the world, calm in the interface, chaos in the rewards.
- Readable portrait-mobile silhouettes at 480x854 with subtle actor outlines and strong foreground/background separation.
- Auto-combat must remain enjoyable to watch; tapping adds meaningful punch without becoming mandatory.
- Loot is physical: chests land, charge, erupt, eject items, settle, then UI summarizes.
- Characters are not weapon-locked; every Lootling may eventually use every weapon family.
- Progression should become visible through Core glow, gear, trails, cosmetics, poses, and evolved weapon silhouettes.
- Offline/local remains a complete game; future online/social systems are additive and server-authoritative.

### Core player promise

**Tiny adorable relic-beasts go into dangerous ruins carrying equipment that looks physically unreasonable, build increasingly ridiculous synergies, and make treasure explode out of the world.**

## 2. World Premise & Long-Term Story


The world is littered with sealed **Relic Vaults** built by a vanished civilisation that stored knowledge, machines, treasure, and something stranger inside crystal **Relic Cores**. A past event called **the Glitterfall** cracked many vaults and scattered Coreglass across the wilderness. Creatures exposed to Coreglass became unusually clever, expressive, and magnetically drawn to relics: the first Lootlings.

Most modern settlements survive by scavenging safe outer ruins. The player's Hideout sits above a newly awakened vault network. When its central Core begins pulsing again, treasure starts surfacing, old guardians wake, and rival creatures migrate toward the signal.

Act I is deliberately small-scale and personal: establish the Hideout, survive the Outer Treasure Ruins, push through the Salvage Warrens, and dislodge the **Hoard Dragon** from the first great vault. Later acts reveal that the dragon was not the source of the awakening. Multiple vault systems are synchronising toward a legendary location called **The First Hoard**, where an ancient governing intelligence known in surviving records as **the Hollow Crown** appears to be calling lost Relic Cores home.

The tone should never become grim-dark. Even the larger mystery is filtered through tiny creatures, improvised equipment, weird merchants, treasure arguments, and charmingly dangerous ruins.

## 3. Playable Lootlings

### Relic Beast — Balanced scavenger / neutral mascot
- **Species / look:** Original relic-beast; cream fur, huge ears and amber eyes, tiny cracked horns, one fang, enormous curled tail
- **Silhouette:** Huge ears + curled tail + tiny horns + glowing chest Core + satchel
- **Palette:** cream, charcoal, amber-gold Core, scavenged olive/brown gear
- **Personality:** Curious, stubborn, opportunistic, delighted by anything shiny.
- **Backstory:** A nameless den-scavenger whose chest Core woke when a buried vault rang beneath the Hideout. It has no famous bloodline or prophecy; it keeps surviving because it pokes everything that glows and runs faster than consequences.
- **Pose language:** Balanced posture; weapon weight is the joke. Ears/tail react to anticipation and rewards.
- **Future passive concept:** Scavenger's Instinct — first treasure interaction each encounter gains a tiny utility bonus, not raw mandatory power.
- **Starter-card pitch:** Balanced, adaptable, and ready for any ridiculous weapon.

### Emberhorn — Brave fire-hearted brawler
- **Species / look:** Fox-dragon relic-beast with swept ember horns, compact muzzle, thick expressive tail
- **Silhouette:** Swept horns + fox ears + flame-tail tuft + broad forearms + hot Core
- **Palette:** burnt orange, dark charcoal, brass, ember-red, white-hot highlights
- **Personality:** Brave, cheeky, impatient, loot-obsessed; treats danger like a dare.
- **Backstory:** Emberhorn hatched in the soot vents beneath an abandoned smelter and learned that most locked doors open if hit hard enough. Its Core burns hotter near ancient metal, which it interprets as treasure saying hello.
- **Pose language:** Chest-forward confidence; strong anticipation crouch; attacks overcommit in a charming way.
- **Future passive concept:** Hot Start — the first damaging hit after a brief lull gains a small fire-flavoured bonus.
- **Starter-card pitch:** Bold, punchy and built for players who like obvious impact.

### Mosskin — Guardian / protector
- **Species / look:** Leaf-eared forest relic-beast with bark plates, mossy mantle and seedpod charms
- **Silhouette:** Leaf mantle + low sturdy stance + bark shoulder plates + broad tail frond
- **Palette:** moss green, bark brown, pale fern, warm jade Core
- **Personality:** Patient, protective, dry sense of humour, secretly competitive.
- **Backstory:** Mosskin guarded a collapsed waystone shrine long after everyone forgot what the shrine was for. When treasure hunters finally arrived, Mosskin decided the safest place for the relics was apparently in its own backpack.
- **Pose language:** Grounded stance, low center of gravity, defensive bracing and protective recovery.
- **Future passive concept:** Rooted Nerve — the first heavy hit taken in an encounter grants a brief modest guard.
- **Starter-card pitch:** Steady and forgiving without being slow or boring.

### Moonkip — Rogue / slippery trickster
- **Species / look:** Long-eared moonlit relic-beast with scarf streamers, compact paws and crescent markings
- **Silhouette:** Long ears + trailing scarf + crescent cheek mark + narrow agile body
- **Palette:** midnight blue, moon-silver, pale cream, cyan-violet Core
- **Personality:** Playful, smug, curious, steals snacks before valuables and denies both.
- **Backstory:** Moonkip grew up inside the roof maze of the old Archive, learning every shortcut and none of the rules. It can hear Relic locks click before they open and has been banned from three vaults it technically discovered.
- **Pose language:** Low crouches, lateral feints, springy recoveries, sharp eye-line toward target.
- **Future passive concept:** Slipstep — periodic attack/reward interactions grant a tiny burst of tempo or avoidance.
- **Starter-card pitch:** Fast, cheeky and visually agile.

### Pebblewyrm — Bruiser / tiny wrecking ball
- **Species / look:** Chunky juvenile stone-wyrm with crystalline back plates, tiny wings and oversized paws
- **Silhouette:** Rock plates + crystal nubs + barrel body + tiny wings + massive paws
- **Palette:** slate, violet crystal, dusty lilac, bright amethyst Core
- **Personality:** Sweet, fearless, food-motivated, thinks every problem is pushable.
- **Backstory:** Pebblewyrm was found asleep inside a geode that miners mistook for treasure. It woke, ate the claim marker, and followed the nearest Lootling home. Nobody has successfully explained property law to it.
- **Pose language:** Strong squash/stretch; attacks begin compressed and end dramatically extended.
- **Future passive concept:** Rolling Momentum — repeated successful hits slowly build a tiny impact bonus that resets when combat pauses.
- **Starter-card pitch:** Funny, chunky and satisfyingly physical.

### Glimmerfin — Mage / elemental tinkerer
- **Species / look:** Axolotl-fin relic-beast with luminous frills, glossy eyes and floating droplet motes
- **Silhouette:** External frills + round tail fin + luminous droplets + floating focus motes
- **Palette:** aqua, sky blue, coral-pink frills, pearly white, cyan Core
- **Personality:** Bright, excitable, easily distracted by magical phenomena, compulsive note-taker.
- **Backstory:** Glimmerfin emerged from a flooded observatory carrying a staff twice its height and absolutely no explanation for how it learned to use it. Its Core hums in harmony with unstable Relic weather.
- **Pose language:** Staff/focus anticipation, floating droplets orbit during charge, elastic magical release.
- **Future passive concept:** Resonance — first status application in an encounter gets a small utility amplification.
- **Starter-card pitch:** Bright spell effects and expressive magical reactions.

### Gloomlet — Assassin / opportunist
- **Species / look:** Small shadow-bat/cat relic-beast with hood-like ears, winged mantle and lantern Core
- **Silhouette:** Bat-cat ears + wing mantle + narrow tail + dangling lantern-like Core
- **Palette:** charcoal, black-plum, magenta-violet, pale eye glow
- **Personality:** Quiet, dramatic, sarcastic, tries very hard to look mysterious while loving praise.
- **Backstory:** Gloomlet lived in the sealed ventilation shafts of Hoard Depths and became convinced it was the terrifying spirit of the vault. The first adventurer who offered it dried fruit ruined the legend permanently.
- **Pose language:** Low stalking posture, vanish-like compression, fast diagonal release, theatrical victory pose.
- **Future passive concept:** Opportunist — modest bonus against already-weakened enemies.
- **Starter-card pitch:** Sneaky, dramatic and built around sharp payoff.

### Runtime pose set required for every playable Lootling

1. Idle/ready  2. Anticipate  3. Attack/release  4. Hurt/recovery  5. Victory/reward. Future optional: run/travel, interact, chest reaction, emote, profile hero pose.

## 4. Zones / Levels / Background & Foreground Direction

- **Placement:** 1–5 / early game
- **Story:** Broken ceremonial streets around the first vault-mouth. Treasure leaks upward in tiny harmless bursts, which is why every scavenger in three valleys has shown up.
- **Background:** Warm moonlit sandstone ruins, tilted pillars, distant treasure glow, blue night sky, hanging vines.
- **Midground:** Broken bridges, relic arches, shallow water channels, collapsed market stalls.
- **Foreground/parallax:** Grass tufts, loose coins, cracked pots, dangling roots, occasional close parallax pillar edge.
- **Palette:** navy night, sandstone gold, teal moss, amber relic light
- **Music:** Curious adventure; warm pluck, light hand drum, distant bell, gentle relic shimmer.
- **Enemy family:** Razor Imp, Pot Gobbler, Ruin Skitter, Shard Bat, Grump Knight

## 5. Enemy Variant System

- **Hasty** — Faster attack rhythm; modestly reduced durability. Visual: forward-leaning pose, speed streak accent. Reward identity: normal.
- **Armoured** — Higher effective durability; slower/heavier presentation. Visual: metal plates, dull silver edge. Reward identity: small Scrip bonus.
- **Volatile** — Telegraphed death burst that lightly punishes standing close. Visual: cracked orange Core, pulsing seams. Reward identity: small Scrap chance.
- **Cursed** — Stronger status/debuff pressure. Visual: violet haze, dark glyph chip. Reward identity: slightly improved Rune weighting.
- **Gilded** — Rare tougher treasure enemy with improved payout. Visual: gold outline accents, coin motes. Reward identity: Gold/Scrap/gear boost.
- **Frostbitten** — Periodic slow/tempo interference, clearly telegraphed. Visual: icy edge and breath. Reward identity: frost-themed loot weight.
- **Overcharged** — Occasional chain spark after attack. Visual: cyan arcs around Core. Reward identity: lightning-themed loot weight.
- **Rootbound** — Starts guarded, loses guard after first break. Visual: roots/bark wrap legs. Reward identity: armour/charm weight.
- **Echoing** — Repeats a weakened version of one attack after a delay. Visual: ghost afterimage. Reward identity: utility affix weight.
- **Hungry** — Grows slightly stronger after another enemy dies in same group. Visual: red eye glint, jaw aura. Reward identity: normal.
- **Hoarder** — Attempts to flee after stealing one loose reward unless stopped. Visual: bulging pouch, coin trail. Reward identity: bonus chest fragment.
- **Shiny** — Very rare cosmetic palette variant; minimal gameplay change. Visual: alternate palette + sparkle. Reward identity: collection credit / cosmetic chance.

### Group encounter archetypes

- **Solo Duelist** — 1 skilled regular. Purpose: readable low-noise fight; showcases variant identity.
- **Heavy Solo** — 1 tank/bruiser. Purpose: weapon impact and dodge/tempo check.
- **Skirmisher Duo** — 2 light enemies. Purpose: target handoff and small group pressure.
- **Guard + Caster** — 1 tank + 1 caster. Purpose: priority target choice.
- **Thief Pair** — 2 evasive treasure thieves. Purpose: reward protection / chase feel.
- **Scraprat Pack** — 3 tiny weak swarmers. Purpose: AoE/proc satisfaction without horde-game density.
- **Summoner + Minion** — 1 support + 1 spawned minor. Purpose: interrupt/priority pressure.
- **Mender Pair** — 1 bruiser + 1 healer. Purpose: clear support enemy readability.
- **Ambush** — 1–2 enemies enter from unusual side after short warning. Purpose: world-event surprise.
- **Treasure Escape** — 1 rare loot creature attempts to flee. Purpose: high-energy optional reward event.
- **Mimic Room** — chest becomes enemy after interaction. Purpose: risk/reward surprise.
- **Elite Guard** — 1 elite + 1 regular. Purpose: late-run tactical spike.

## 6. Weapon Families, Correct Posing, Angles & Ammo

**Global rule:** weapon silhouettes and body posture must explain the weapon before text does. Projectiles originate from the weapon, not the character centre. Any Lootling may eventually use any family.

### Greatsword — heavy melee
- **Held/attack angle:** rest 25° back over shoulder; wind-up 45° rear; impact diagonal down-forward
- **Ammo/projectile:** none
- **Motion:** load back → huge diagonal cleave → recoil settle
- **Base examples:** Rustcleaver, Moonsteel Slab, Mimic Blade, Grump Greatblade
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Rustcleaver → Vaultcleaver → Crowncleaver
  - Moonsteel Slab → Starbreaker → Sky-Sunder
  - Mimic Blade → Hungry Edge → Maw of Plenty

### Greataxe — heavy melee
- **Held/attack angle:** head high at 35°; two-hand body lean
- **Ammo/projectile:** none
- **Motion:** overhead/side chop with pronounced follow-through
- **Base examples:** Scrap Axe, Rootsplitter, Coinchop, Kiln Reaver
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Scrap Axe → Furnace Axe → Cinder Crown
  - Rootsplitter → Wildsplitter → Worldsplitter
  - Coinchop → Gilded Guillotine → Hoard Harvester

### Maul — impact melee
- **Held/attack angle:** head behind body at 20° until wind-up; impact near-horizontal/descending
- **Ammo/projectile:** none
- **Motion:** compress → swing → ground-shock settle
- **Base examples:** Mimic Mallet, Pebble Pounder, Bell Maul, Scrap Sledge
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Mimic Mallet → Vault Mallet → Hoardhammer
  - Pebble Pounder → Faultmaker → Worldknocker
  - Bell Maul → Resonant Maul → Cathedral Bell

### Spear / Halberd — reach melee
- **Held/attack angle:** 10–15° upward toward enemy, rear hand near hip
- **Ammo/projectile:** none
- **Motion:** brace → straight thrust or sweeping hook → snap recovery
- **Base examples:** Ruin Pike, Moonhook, Thorn Lance, Gear Halberd
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Ruin Pike → Vault Pike → First Spear
  - Moonhook → Crescent Hook → Eclipse Halberd
  - Thorn Lance → Briar Lance → Verdant Needle

### Twin Daggers — fast melee
- **Held/attack angle:** one blade forward 20°, offhand blade reverse-grip near hip
- **Ammo/projectile:** none
- **Motion:** low crouch → crossing slash → spring back
- **Base examples:** Moonbite Daggers, Scrap Shivs, Gilded Knives, Mossfang Pair
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Moonbite Daggers → Moonfangs → Night's Receipt
  - Scrap Shivs → Rivet Fangs → Geargrin
  - Gilded Knives → King's Change → Crown Tax

### Claws / Gauntlets — fast melee
- **Held/attack angle:** integrated on paws; elbows low
- **Ammo/projectile:** none
- **Motion:** double swipe / uppercut with full body lean
- **Base examples:** Ruin Claws, Cinder Knuckles, Shade Claws, Barkfist
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Ruin Claws → Vault Talons → Relic Rippers
  - Cinder Knuckles → Furnace Fists → Caldera Hands
  - Shade Claws → Gloom Talons → Null Rake

### Bow — ranged precision
- **Held/attack angle:** bow vertical/slightly canted 5–10°; arrow nocked toward target; never backwards
- **Ammo/projectile:** arrow
- **Motion:** brace → draw → release recoil; damage on arrow arrival
- **Base examples:** Crooked Bow, Moonbranch, Gilded Shortbow, Warrens Longbow
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Crooked Bow → Ruin Longbow → Starstring
  - Moonbranch → Crescent Bow → Fullmoon Arc
  - Gilded Shortbow → Hoardbow → King's Dividend

### Crossbow — ranged burst
- **Held/attack angle:** mostly horizontal, stock anchored near shoulder/chest
- **Ammo/projectile:** bolt
- **Motion:** raise → tiny lock/click → bolt launch → mechanical recoil
- **Base examples:** Scrap Repeater, Vault Crossbow, Needlecaster, Cinder Boltthrower
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Scrap Repeater → Gear Repeater → Storm Repeater
  - Vault Crossbow → Lockbreaker → Door Argument
  - Needlecaster → Quillstorm → Index Eraser

### Relic Sling — ranged arc
- **Held/attack angle:** low circular wind-up beside body
- **Ammo/projectile:** stone/relic pellet/bomb
- **Motion:** spin → release arcing projectile → hand follow-through
- **Base examples:** Pebble Sling, Coin Slinger, Spore Sling, Glass Pellet Sling
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Pebble Sling → Geode Sling → Meteor Pocket
  - Coin Slinger → Goldspinner → Treasury Problem
  - Spore Sling → Bloomlobber → Mossburst

### Staff — ranged magic
- **Held/attack angle:** 25–35° forward/up; focus tip clear of face
- **Ammo/projectile:** magic orb / element projectile
- **Motion:** plant/raise → Core/focus charge → arcing cast → magical recoil
- **Base examples:** Glimmerstaff, Cinder Staff, Moss Staff, Shardseer Staff
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Glimmerstaff → Starstaff → Observatory Spine
  - Cinder Staff → Furnace Rod → Crownfire Branch
  - Moss Staff → Verdant Rod → Root of the Vault

### Wand — fast magic
- **Held/attack angle:** 15° toward target, elbow bent
- **Ammo/projectile:** bolt/spark
- **Motion:** quick point/flick → fast projectile → tiny wrist recoil
- **Base examples:** Ruin Wand, Bubble Wand, Hexstick, Bell Wand
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Ruin Wand → Vault Wand → First Spark
  - Bubble Wand → Tide Wand → Deepglow
  - Hexstick → Cursebranch → Null Needle

### Orb / Focus — magic control
- **Held/attack angle:** floating 15–30 px from paw/chest; hand guides rather than grips
- **Ammo/projectile:** beam/orb/shards
- **Motion:** orbit compresses → focus flashes → projectile/beam → orbit expands
- **Base examples:** Relic Orb, Mirror Focus, Storm Pearl, Ink Sphere
- **Evolution lines:**
  - Relic Orb → Vault Sphere → Core Satellite
  - Mirror Focus → Echo Focus → Unchosen Eye
  - Storm Pearl → Tempest Pearl → Skyheart

### Projectile presentation profiles

- **arrow (Bow):** angle direct line to target; slight 1–2° upward visual bias; speed fast; silhouette long thin shaft + bright fletching tip; impact small stick/spark.
- **bolt (Crossbow):** angle direct horizontal; speed very fast; silhouette short thick bolt; impact hard mechanical thunk/spark.
- **sling_stone (Relic Sling):** angle visible parabolic arc; speed medium; silhouette round stone/relic pellet; impact dust/chip burst.
- **magic_orb (Staff):** angle gentle arc from staff tip; speed medium; silhouette glowing sphere with short trail; impact element burst.
- **wand_spark (Wand):** angle direct/light curve; speed fast; silhouette small bright dart; impact sharp spark.
- **focus_shard (Orb/Focus):** angle spiral/offset launch; speed medium-fast; silhouette crystal shard or energy crescent; impact clean pulse.

## 13. UI / Menu / HUD Art Direction

- **Rule:** Life in the world, calm in the interface, chaos in the rewards.
- **Combat Hud:** Top-left/upper region reserved for compact wave/zone identity; HP/XP anchored and minimal; statuses use chips; build strip collapses outside combat; battlefield remains visible.
- **Menus:** Prefer physical scene art + floating cards/chips over full-screen opaque rectangles. One hero focal point per screen.
- **Trophies:** Badge-first gallery with category tabs, recent unlock hero card, progress rings/bars, locked silhouettes, secret question marks.
- **Inventory:** Item art first, name/rarity second, 2–3 headline effects, compare/details expandable.
- **Starter Select:** Large character hero art, 5-pose preview carousel, short personality/playstyle, lock reason if unavailable.
- **Loading:** Short illustrated postcard/scene with one quip/tip; never fake a loading wait.

## 14. Audio / VFX Direction

- **Music:** Original long-form location identity; avoid fatiguing exposed sine loops in production. Use adventure motifs that can recur across zones.
- **Combat SFX:** Multiple variants per frequent event; emphasize body/transient at low-mid frequencies; limit high-pitch repetition.
- **Common chest:** short wooden click/thump + tiny sparkle.
- **Rare chest:** low resonance + quick ascending 2–3-note cue + blue burst.
- **Epic chest:** rumble + rising harmonic layer + brief duck + violet burst sting.
- **Legendary chest:** deep treasure resonance → rising tonal/percussive build → 100–200 ms micro-silence → wide gold impact + melodic sting; never harsh or clipped.
- **Actor readability:** subtle local actor outline; stronger only for elite/boss. No full-screen outline shader.
- **Common:** silver-white
- **Rare:** blue-cyan
- **Epic:** violet-purple
- **Legendary:** gold-amber-orange
- **Mythic:** white-gold with dark-star accent
