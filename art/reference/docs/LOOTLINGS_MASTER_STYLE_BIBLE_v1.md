# LOOTLINGS — MASTER STYLE BIBLE v1

## North Star
**Cute first. Badass second. Stylish/confident third. Fantasy always.**

Lootlings should feel collectible and adorable at first glance, then reveal darker, sharper, more dangerous fantasy personality underneath.

High-level inspiration pillars:
- **Overwatch 1/2:** silhouette readability, character appeal, expressive personality, skin-worthy presentation.
- **Diablo:** dark-fantasy richness, demonic/corrupted energy, premium loot fantasy, gothic menace, reward spectacle.
- **D&D / LOTR / The Witcher:** grounded fantasy materials, believable relic history, monsters, factions, armor, ruins, magic and worldbuilding.
- **Borderlands:** punchy loot identity, exaggerated personality, strong reward feedback and memorable presentation.

Use the design lessons only. Lootlings must remain original and must not copy protected characters, franchise-specific silhouettes, UI, logos, music, sound effects, maps, weapon designs or compositions.

## Player Promise
Within the first 30 seconds the game should communicate:
1. This world is magical.
2. Loot matters.
3. These creatures are cute but dangerous.
4. Gear changes how I feel and fight.
5. Rare things look and sound rare.
6. The game has personality.
7. I want to see what I unlock next.

If a system exists but the player cannot see, hear or feel it, it is not finished from a presentation standpoint.

## Characters
- Small, chunky, collectible bodies.
- Strong silhouettes at 480x854.
- Large expressive face/head features.
- One iconic visual hook per character.
- Oversized or highly readable gear.
- Avoid tiny decorative noise that disappears in gameplay.

Every major character should have one instantly readable personality trait, one signature material/element, one strong silhouette feature, one memorable combat read and one obvious future skin hook.

“Sexy” in Lootlings means appealing, confident, stylish, charismatic and cool — never explicit, tacky, or at odds with the cute collectible identity.

Future skin families can include Infernal, Celestial, Corrupted, Relic Knight, Witch-Hunter, Rogue, Necrotic, Moonlit, Festival, Noble, Graveborn, Arcane, Emberforged, Frostbound, Plague and Treasure Hoarder.

## Weapons
Every weapon must read instantly at mobile scale.
- Strong silhouette before detail.
- Grip and attack direction obvious.
- Distinct family identity.
- Exaggerate the important functional part.
- Avoid thin forms that disappear at 480x854.
- Animation and projectile origin must make physical sense.

Preferred materials: relic gold, blackened iron, dark steel, obsidian, demon/dragon bone, cursed carved wood, crystal, moonstone, ember cores, rune-metal, enchanted leather, alchemical glass, corrupted resin and silvered hunter metal.

Rarity:
- Common: functional, restrained, minimal glow.
- Rare: stronger material/color identity, subtle magic.
- Epic: unique silhouette accents, animated runes/glow, stronger personality.
- Legendary: aspirational silhouette, signature VFX, premium sound, unmistakable visual story.

A Legendary should create: **“Holy shit, what is THAT?”**

## World / Environments
Blend whimsical collectible fantasy with dangerous dark-fantasy spaces.

Strong location families:
- cursed forests
- moonlit ruins
- relic vaults
- demon caverns
- ruined sanctums
- ember forges
- plague swamps
- crystal grottos
- old hunter villages
- celestial halls
- treasure hoards
- fungal caverns
- forgotten libraries
- corrupted gardens

Backgrounds can be rich, but actors, projectiles, loot, hazards and interactables must always win the visual hierarchy.

## HUD / UI / Menus
Premium fantasy interface — never programmer panels.

Use relic-metal edges, carved frames, rune accents, crystal details, magical glass, rarity-driven glow, subtle motes, tactile buttons and clear iconography.

Avoid endless stacked rectangles, flat debug bars, giant text walls, spreadsheet layouts, tiny type and decorative clutter that hurts scanning.

Every screen should have one obvious focal point, one primary action, concise secondary information, a clear back/close action and minimal competing noise.

Combat HUD prioritizes health, immediate danger, wave/progress, relevant resource and current build identity. The fight remains visually dominant.

## Loot / Chests / RNG / Rewards
Loot is a core emotional pillar.

Rarity escalation:
- Common: quick, clean, satisfying.
- Rare: visible glow, stronger impact, distinct sound.
- Epic: anticipation, premium framing, stronger particles.
- Legendary: controlled wind-up, brief pause, strong light/rune burst, premium sting and unmistakable payoff.

Chest sequence:
1. idle presence
2. anticipation
3. latch/wind-up
4. light leak
5. brief pause
6. open burst
7. item reveal
8. rarity celebration
9. clean decision state

RNG should feel exciting, not manipulative. No fake spin outcomes or misleading near-misses.

## VFX / Particles
Reusable vocabulary:
- relic sparks
- rune arcs
- crystal shards
- dust motes
- magical trails
- ember flicker
- shadow wisps
- celestial motes
- necrotic mist
- impact flashes
- restrained screen shake

Gameplay communication first, beauty second. Avoid particle soup and bloom everywhere.

## Audio
Audio should make Lootlings feel expensive.

Target SFX:
- tactile UI click
- premium confirm/back
- melee contact
- arrow release/impact
- magical attack identity
- player hurt
- enemy defeat
- loot pickup
- chest anticipation
- rarity stings
- shrine/progression confirmation

Tonal palette: magical relic energy, dark fantasy, metallic treasure, arcane shimmer, demonic weight, organic monster textures and occasional playful creature character.

Use only original, generated, CC0, properly licensed royalty-free, or otherwise commercially safe audio with provenance recorded. Never use ripped or recognizable sounds/music from commercial games.

## Music
Support dark fantasy mystery, magical adventure, demonic pressure, heroic momentum, treasure/loot excitement, eerie ruins and triumphant reward moments.

Do not fill silence just because space exists. Leave room for combat SFX and UI readability.

## Materials / Currency / Icons
Core visual materials:
- relic gold
- dark iron
- violet crystal
- ember orange
- moonlit silver
- corrupted purple/black
- arcane cyan
- bone/ivory
- worn wood/leather

Currencies and collectible icons should look like physical fantasy objects, not flat app icons.

## Bosses / Elites
Bosses need instantly readable silhouettes, stronger telegraphs, unique sound identity, unique reward expectation, arena presence and visible escalation over normal enemies.

## Cosmetics
Support cute, badass, infernal, celestial, corrupted, spooky, noble, rogue, hunter, festival, arcane, treasure-hoarder, witch/warlock and relic-knight themes.

## Production Rule
When choosing between more hidden systems and making an existing system look/feel significantly better, prefer the player-facing improvement unless the hidden work is genuinely required.

Every major pass should answer:
- What did the player SEE improve?
- What did the player HEAR improve?
- What did the player FEEL improve?

## Quality Gate
Before calling a visual/audio feature complete:
- inspect it in the real rendered build
- inspect at native 480x854
- compare against this Style Bible
- verify readability
- verify animation orientation/attachment
- verify audio level and repetition
- verify commercial-use provenance
- verify it does not imitate protected franchise-specific assets

Lootlings should increasingly feel like one coherent original game — not a collection of unrelated cool assets.
