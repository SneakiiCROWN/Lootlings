## Service separation
Power:
Shrine = controlled meta progression
Forge = weapon evolution/crafting
Pack Peddler = run gear
Salvager = stash/scrap/cache

Cosmetic:
Wardrobe = outfits/hair/dyes/accessories
Appearance Station = face/body presentation
Practice = free preview

Fashion must not become power.

# World spine
Aurel Reach stored promises inside Relic Cores. The First Hoard preserved shared obligations until the last Crown tried to own them. Crownfall shattered roads, vaults and memories. Lootlings now return what the old world failed to protect.

## Factions
Lantern Compact — independent scavengers/merchants, safe routes, rescue debts.
Quillkeepers — archivists/relic scholars; truth versus exploitation.
Hollow Court — surviving ownership ideology, collectors, tribute-takers.
The Unwritten — erased/corrupted memories and people; strange does not automatically mean evil.

## Side-story format
Use 3-beat Field Note chains:
1. discovery/problem
2. contradictory witness
3. choice/reveal
Rewards may be lore, cosmetics, titles/banners, existing currencies or existing item pools. Not every story needs power.
