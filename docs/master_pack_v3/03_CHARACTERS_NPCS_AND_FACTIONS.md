# Characters, NPCs and Factions Expansion Bible

Existing canonical residents remain authoritative. New names below are **planned** and should not be reported as shipped until implemented.

## Core resident cast

### Patch Brasswick — Salvager / Forge hand
Adult. Dry, competent, practical. Rebuilt the return-gate and treats every impossible weapon as a repair ticket. His shop should feel cluttered, warm and mechanically useful. He comments on newly forged weapon families and particularly distrusts ornate things that cannot survive being dropped.

### Ilex Quill — Archivist
Adult. Precise, darkly funny, deeply protective of names and records. Her Archive dialogue should alternate between unsettling history and tiny deadpan jokes. She remembers player discoveries and regional bosses.

### Sera Vey — Relic Keeper
Adult. Warm, firm, spiritually curious without being naïve. She opposes using living Cores as commodities. Shrine lines should make the world feel older and stranger while still being human.

### Merrow Vane — Pack Peddler
Adult. Cheerful courier-merchant with a huge pack and suspiciously good timing. He is evasive about routes but reliable about rescue debts. He can anchor travelling shop scenes and route-room encounters.

## Planned recurring side cast

### Nessa Vale — Lantern Huntress
Adult Reachborn woman; any final complexion can be chosen during production. Crimson/black field gear, hood/cape options, bow/crossbow. Competitive, sharp, not cruel. Tracks false lanterns and missing travellers. Can serve as rival, quest giver and optional route ally.

### Corvin Ash — Broken Collector
Adult Ashmarked man; broad or muscular frame. Former Hollow Court tribute collector. Blackened armour, red scarf, horn/scar variants. Gruff and ashamed, not a generic evil edgelord. Story theme: strength used to own people versus strength used to protect them.

### Vesper Thread — Nullglass Witch
Adult Veilborn woman; glamorous occult presentation, violet/black/gold. Researches abandoned futures. Ominous in public, unexpectedly gentle in private. Strong dialogue partner for Gloomlet and Ilex.

### Rook Marr — Gate Warden
Adult Reachborn nonbinary veteran; practical plate/leather. Manages dangerous departures, route contracts and rescue rules. Dry professionalism; hates dramatic last words because they create paperwork.

### Tamsin Coil — Gearworks Engineer
Adult Gearworks tinker; goggles, rolled sleeves, brass prosthetic/tool rig optional. Curious, sarcastic, always halfway through three repairs. Shop/quest role around projectiles, crossbows, slings, traps and machinery.

### Edda Sable — Clothier / Glamour Smith
Adult tailor, stylist and relic-fabric specialist. This is the main diegetic home for outfit appearance, dyes, hairstyles, makeup/war paint presets and glamour skins. Edda treats "practical" and "hot" as perfectly compatible design goals.

### Brother Kelm — Field Cook
Adult, large friendly presence, apron over armour remnants. Runs communal meal buffs only if/when mechanics support it; until then he is story flavour and a social NPC. Excellent source of jokes, rumours and region anecdotes.

### Pippa Voss — Contract Broker
Adult. Fast-talking coordinator for optional objectives. Keeps contract UI grounded in a character rather than a floating spreadsheet.

## Ordinary NPC pool

To make hubs feel inhabited without over-writing every background character, build reusable archetypes:
- scavenger worker
- caravan guard
- wounded hunter
- archivist junior
- shrine attendant
- forge apprentice
- courier
- cook/helper
- child NPCs may exist in safe social scenes only; never use adult outfit systems on them
- trader
- refugee
- expedition veteran
- masked Hollow Court defector
- travelling musician

Each archetype needs 3–6 short barks and 2–3 appearance variants before unique named lore is necessary.

## Factions

### Lantern Compact
Independent scavengers, route guards, couriers and merchants. Visuals: amber lanterns, patched leather, red cloth, brass fittings.

### Quillkeepers
Archivists and relic scholars. Visuals: navy, parchment, violet crystal, restrained gold.

### Hollow Court
Collectors enforcing the broken Crown's ownership claims. Visuals: blackened metal, crimson, crowns/chains, infernal ornament.

### Unwritten
Corrupted memories, altered creatures and erased people. Not automatically evil. Visuals vary by region; use broken symmetry and "missing" shapes rather than one generic purple faction.

### Free Companies
Planned umbrella for player-friendly hunters, mercenaries and wanderers. Allows humanoid player backgrounds without making every hero a member of the same guild.

## Villain / boss personality rule

Every major boss should answer three questions:
1. What promise/purpose was twisted?
2. What do they want right now?
3. What visual behaviour communicates that before text?

Do not rely on "big demon = evil" alone.

Examples already established:
- Verdant Mirror: guardianship twisted into possession.
- Furnace Regent: rescue warmth twisted into tribute.
- Hollow Crown: shared trust twisted into ownership.
