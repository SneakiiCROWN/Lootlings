# Audio Source Shortlist — Free / Low-Cost Beta Options

This is a sourcing shortlist, not an instruction to dump random stock audio into the build.

## Preferred SFX source: SONNISS GameAudioGDC archive

Why:
- professional game-audio material;
- current bundle license allows commercial use;
- royalty-free;
- modification allowed;
- attribution not required;
- suitable for games/interactive projects.

Use for:
- weapon impacts;
- fire/ice/electric/stone;
- chains/gears/doors;
- chest mechanics;
- ambience layers;
- creature textures;
- UI source material after editing.

Important:
- record the exact bundle/year and source file in provenance;
- do not redistribute the raw library;
- design/layer sounds so Lootlings develops its own sonic identity.

## Secondary simple SFX source: Kenney

Kenney's asset-page game assets are CC0 and commercially usable without attribution. Useful for placeholder/simple UI or utility cues, but do not let a generic pack dictate the final sonic identity.

## Music beta shortlist

### Lisette Amago — Free Dark Fantasy Music
Free/name-your-price dark-fantasy loops; advertised royalty-free and YouTube-safe. Good candidate for temporary dark-fantasy region/menu evaluation.

### Yugen Mukaido — Crimson Rose of the Labyrinth
Free commercial-use gothic/orchestral/metal boss track with loopable version. Strong candidate for a **temporary boss-track benchmark**, especially for Cinder Crown / high-intensity encounters. Do not make one external track the permanent Lootlings theme without deciding the broader soundtrack identity.

### Asakumo — Free BGM packs
Recent free packs with commercial use and monetization allowed, including dark dungeons, volcano areas, ice temples and mid-boss material. Useful for prototyping region contrast.

### Pixabay Audio
Current content license permits free use, adaptation and commercial use subject to its license/prohibited uses. Useful for supplementary ambience/music tests, but keep download/source records because individual content may involve additional rights and music can still trigger platform claims.

## Recommended actual workflow

1. Replace the most annoying **UI/SFX** first using designed layers from SONNISS / CC0 material.
2. Build one coherent temporary soundtrack set for:
   - Hideout
   - general route/combat
   - dark/occult region
   - fire/hell region
   - ice region
   - boss
3. Compare all tracks against the Relic Motif direction in `15_AUDIO_MUSIC_AND_SOUND_DIRECTION.md`.
4. Keep only tracks that sound like the same game.
5. Record license/source metadata before import.
6. Normalize/edit loops and transitions.
7. Run a 20–30 minute repetition-fatigue playtest.
8. Later replace temporary music with a coherent original/commissioned soundtrack when budget permits.

## Do not

- rip music from commercial games;
- use fan remixes;
- use YouTube uploads without explicit licensing;
- assume "copyright free" means commercial-game-safe;
- ship unedited random fantasy tracks from ten creators if they clash;
- delete existing volume-bus/settings architecture just to replace the source files.
