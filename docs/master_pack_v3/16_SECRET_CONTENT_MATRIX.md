## Secret reward philosophy

Preferred rewards:
- lore entry
- profile title
- cosmetic
- banner
- unusual dye
- emote
- room variant
- rare NPC line
- soundtrack layer
- Archive illustration

Avoid mandatory combat power hidden behind obscure puzzles.

## Community puzzle cadence

A good mystery chain should contain:
- an obvious first hook;
- two corroborating clues;
- one misleading but fair interpretation;
- one cross-region connection;
- one satisfying reveal;
- one remaining unanswered question.

Do not make every secret a number cipher. Use visual, audio, spatial, dialogue and item-lore clues too.
