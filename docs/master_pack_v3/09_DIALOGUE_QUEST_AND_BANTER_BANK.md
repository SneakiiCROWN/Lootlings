## Quest/Field Note seeds
### The Road That Returned
Aurelia chain. A crystal map shows a road erased from every paper chart.
1. recover the survey crystal;
2. compare Ilex's record with a surviving courier mark;
3. discover the road was intentionally removed to hide a rescue route.
Reward direction: title/banner/cosmetic, not mandatory power.

### The Warmest Debt
Emberhorn + Patch.
A rescue furnace still recognises an old emergency promise.
Resolve whether its last stored heat belongs to a vault, a village or nobody.

### Names in the Margin
Ilex + Gloomlet.
Anonymous corrections appear in erased Archive pages.
Reveal that recognition can survive official deletion.

### The False Lantern
Nessa/Moonfen.
Three lights claim to lead home; only one remembers why travellers left.
Story-unlock direction for Nessa.

### Ash Owed
Corvin/Cinder Crown.
Return items taken as tribute to families or institutions that still exist.
Story-unlock direction for Corvin.

### A Future Refused
Vesper/Nullglass.
Recover a reflection she deliberately abandoned because it required someone else's sacrifice.
Story-unlock direction for Vesper.

### Person, Machine, Memory
Juno/Gearworks.
A machine remembers habits and jokes but no name.
The quest asks what evidence is enough to treat a memory with care.

### The Bell That Didn't Ring
Moonkip/Frostbell.
A missing warning chime was never stolen; someone silenced it to protect a hiding place.

### A Better Shield
Mosskin.
A village keeps repairing the same watchstone instead of asking why danger always reaches it.

### Encore for Nobody
Glimmerfin.
A drowned concert hall still performs to empty seats. Recover the audience register, not just the instrument.

## Story screen principles
Short combat/transition text: 3–10 words.
Dialogue: generally 1–2 compact bubbles.
Field Notes: optional longer reading.
Never block combat for lore unless the player deliberately opened it.
