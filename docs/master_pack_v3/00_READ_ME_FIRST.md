## Locked product DNA
Cute first, badass second — but the world can also be sexy, glamorous, horrific, warm, funny and brutal.
- Lootlings remain mascots/core playable identities.
- Adult human/humanoid playable characters are added alongside them, not instead of them.
- Appearance is cosmetic and separate from power.
- Adult customisation supports broad skin tones, gender presentation, body frames, hair, facial hair, makeup, scars, tattoos/warpaint, outfits, jewellery, dyes, poses and accessories.
- Some adult looks may be practical, glamorous, daring/raunchy or heavily armoured; all sexualised humanoids are clearly adults.
- Weapons stay separate from actor bodies and must align using weapon-family grip/socket/stance rules.
- Existing save IDs, economy, loot odds and validated combat formulas are protected until a dedicated balance checkpoint explicitly changes them.

## Inspiration guardrail
Use principles only:
- Witcher 3: lived-in monster hunting, grounded materials, side-story texture.
- Diablo: infernal/cathedral grandeur, loot drama, threatening silhouettes.
- DOOM: demon scale, aggressive readability, heavy-metal energy.
- Borderlands: weapon personality, loot readability, oddball NPC confidence.
- Skyrim: ruins, exploration, factions, environmental storytelling.
- LOTR: ancient-road history, fellowship warmth, old-world scale.
- Overwatch 1/2: readable silhouettes, personality-forward skins.
- Baldur's Gate 3: broad adult character expression, attractive varied cast, player agency.
Everything remains original Lootlings.

## Critical implementation rule
A concept board is not a runtime asset merely because it looks good. New content needs a stable ID, an honest art path, save-safe data and real phone-size QA before it is "live".
