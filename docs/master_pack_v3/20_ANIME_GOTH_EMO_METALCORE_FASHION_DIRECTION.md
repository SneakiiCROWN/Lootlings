# Lootlings — Anime / Goth / Emo / Metalcore Fashion Direction

Status: APPROVED CREATIVE REFERENCE  
Scope: adult human/humanoid Wanderers, adult NPCs, cosmetics, hair, makeup, jewellery, tattoos, social outfits, combat outfits, promotional art and selected UI/profile presentation.

## Purpose

Lootlings should deliberately include an alternative-fashion lane inspired by anime character appeal, gothic fashion, emo styling, metal/metalcore stagewear and dark-fantasy glamour.

This is NOT the only visual lane. It sits beside practical adventurer, elegant, rugged, cute, heroic, demonic, heavily armoured, nature, arcane and other styles.

All human/humanoid characters referenced by this document are adults.

## Core feel

Aim for combinations of:
- sexy / glamorous;
- goth / dark romantic;
- emo / alt;
- metal / metalcore;
- anime-influenced silhouette and expression;
- badass combat-readiness;
- occasionally cute or playful accents;
- infernal / occult / crystalline fantasy motifs.

The goal is "I want to play as them / unlock that outfit / screenshot that look", not generic fantasy costume filler.

## Important originality rule

Use genre and subculture design principles only. Do NOT copy:
- existing anime characters;
- real musicians or bands;
- protected logos;
- signature costumes from another game;
- album artwork;
- exact tattoos, symbols or merchandise designs.

Band/scene influence should come through silhouette, styling, layering, attitude, materials and mood.

## Adult sexy / raunchy spectrum

Adult cosmetics may range from conservative to deliberately revealing.

Allowed visual lanes include:
- exposed midriff;
- deep necklines;
- crop tops;
- fitted leather;
- corset-inspired armour;
- thigh-high boots;
- short skirts/shorts with fantasy armour layering;
- open shirts/jackets;
- harness-inspired fashion elements;
- mesh/lace-inspired opaque fantasy layering;
- asymmetric straps;
- exposed shoulders/back;
- shirtless or partially open-chest masculine looks;
- fitted trousers;
- body jewellery;
- glam stagewear;
- dark romantic formalwear.

Keep anatomy covered appropriately for a mainstream commercial game. Sexy should read as confident adult fashion, never as coerced or demeaning presentation.

## Gender / body / skin variety

Do not collapse this aesthetic into one pale thin woman.

Create attractive alternative-fashion options across:
- women;
- men;
- masculine, feminine and androgynous presentation;
- varied skin tones;
- varied facial structures;
- lean, athletic, broad, muscular, soft and heavier builds where the production system supports them;
- varied ages within an adult cast.

A sexy goth guy, muscular alt woman, elegant dark-skinned witch, pretty androgynous rogue, huge tattooed metal bruiser and cute emo tinkerer can all belong in Lootlings.

## Outfit families

Recommended alt-fashion cosmetic families:

### 1. Nightstage
Metalcore/stagewear energy: black leather, layered straps, distressed cloth, boots, silver hardware, crystal accent colour.

### 2. Cathedral Black
Gothic romantic: long coats, corset shapes, dark velvet, lace-like fantasy patterning, gold filigree, high collars, dramatic sleeves.

### 3. Heartbreak Static
Emo/alt streetwear: fitted tees/crops, layered sleeves, belts, chains, boots, asymmetric hair, red/purple/cyan accents.

### 4. Infernal Encore
Sexy demonic performance look: horn motifs, blackened metal, glowing red seams, leather, sharp silhouettes, optional exposed torso/midriff.

### 5. Moon Mourner
Elegant dark witch/warlock lane: deep navy/black/violet, silver jewellery, moons, sheer-looking but opaque fantasy fabrics, flowing shapes.

### 6. Riot Relic
Battle-ready alt adventurer: practical base layers plus punk/metal hardware, relic plates, damaged coat, straps, combat boots.

### 7. Cute Catastrophe
Cute + alt: oversized jacket, playful accessories, dark palette with bright crystal accents, charms, stitched patches, expressive silhouette.

### 8. Black Halo
High-fashion endgame goth: ornate black/gold armour-fashion hybrid, dramatic cape or coat tails, luminous relic jewellery.

## Armour adaptation

Alternative fashion must still read correctly when armour is equipped.

Use modular visual rules:
- retain hair/face/piercing identity where clipping permits;
- armour can replace torso/leg layers while preserving style accents;
- dyes/accent crystals should carry character palette;
- avoid making every armour set erase the player's chosen identity;
- heavier armour should have goth/metal variants, not only generic knight plate.

## Weapon pairing

Alt-fashion characters must work with the same gameplay weapon families.

Do NOT hard-bake a weapon into the base body art.

Weapon presentation should use the shared hand/socket/stance system.

Useful stance flavours:
- relaxed shoulder carry;
- low ready;
- two-hand heavy stance;
- spellcasting hand-out stance;
- confident hip stance;
- dual-weapon pose;
- ranged draw/aim;
- staff grounded pose;
- oversized weapon drag/rest pose.

The appearance choice must never alter weapon stats.

## UI/profile presentation

Character/profile screens may reinforce the aesthetic through:

- subtle stage/backlight;

- animated embers or dust;
- jewellery/wardrobe preview zoom;
- pose preview;
- outfit favourites;

Avoid illegible "edgy" fonts. Core gameplay text must remain highly readable.

## Production checklist

When generating or approving adult Wanderer/NPC fashion art, ask:
1. Does this look like Lootlings rather than generic fantasy art?
2. Is the character clearly adult?
3. Is the silhouette readable at phone size?
4. Does the outfit belong to a named visual lane?
5. Is it distinct from existing accepted presets?
6. Does it preserve broad gender/skin/body variety?
7. Can it work with shared weapon sockets/stances?
8. Can armour layers replace it cleanly?
9. Is the sexiness/glamour deliberate rather than accidental?
10. Is it original and free of copied logos/characters/band imagery?

## Codex implementation rule

Treat this document as a creative reference and content contract, NOT permission to overwrite existing accepted characters.

New presets should be additive and data-driven. Preserve accepted Wanderers and Lootlings. Generate/import new looks as replaceable beta assets until explicitly promoted to accepted/final status.
