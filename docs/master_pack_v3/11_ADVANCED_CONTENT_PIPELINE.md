# ADVANCED CONTENT PIPELINE — AHEAD OF CURRENT NEEDS

This is the prepared runway. It does not mean every item is immediately implemented.

## Near-term
- seeded route/room mode already in active Codex development;
- humanoid avatar kind + origin presets;
- reusable weapon sockets/poses;
- character creator;
- wardrobe/dye/transmog;
- cosmetic Hideout services;
- final Gloomlet and human production bodies.

### Hairstyle unlocks
Mostly available at creator start.
A few region-themed accessories can unlock cosmetically through play.
Do not lock ordinary representation options behind grind.

### Companion presentation
Keep roadmap power limits.
Companions can wear tiny accessories, trails and nameplates.
Do not turn required combat strength into paid fashion pressure.

### Relationships/bonds
Optional dialogue affinity can unlock:
banter
Field Notes
camp scenes
titles
cosmetics
portraits
No forced romance.
If romance is ever added, adult humanoids only and player-choice driven.

Useful for Player Cards and social sharing later.

## Monetisation future guardrail
Current game remains without real-money systems.
If cosmetic monetisation is ever evaluated:
- transparent direct purchase beats paid RNG;
- ordinary skin tones/hair/body representation must not be monetised;
- no pay-to-win power;
- no sexualised youthful designs;
- earned cosmetics remain meaningful;
- region/platform law and store rules require a dedicated future review.
