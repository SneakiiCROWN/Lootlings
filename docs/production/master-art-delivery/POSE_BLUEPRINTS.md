# State production blueprints

These rules accompany, never override, entity anatomy and exact family contacts in each master cell. Gloomlet Bow has more specific per-state instructions embedded in its16 exact cells.

## idle

Balanced ready/neutral, distinct relaxed shoulders and stable hips; full silhouette and equipment contact; no full drawn attack or stride.

## movement

Visible locomotion step/hover propulsion appropriate to source anatomy. Ground-bearing foot or hover reference remains at baseline; trailing anatomy counterbalances. Maintain carried equipment contact, no idle renamed.

## windup

Readable anticipation before attack: load shoulders/hips toward rear brace, contact hands drive real equipment, head attends target. Bow drawing fingers pinch string near cheek; paired blades both readable; caster charge originates at hand/head of staff.

## strike_or_cast

Actual committed attack/release: weight moves toward target, body/arms drive weapon or charge, contact remains believable. Bow rear fingers visibly OPEN, string released and arrow no longer nocked. Paired strike alternates hands with second blade present.

## impact

Actual attack contact instant, not a new neutral pose. Keep target-driven eyes, planted/counterbalancing anatomy and contact module; effects are separate.

## follow_through

Continue the same attack arc/release beyond commit, soften supporting joints and keep equipment attached. Bow rear hand remains open and behind shoulder; do not close draw fist. No new strike relabelled.

## recovery

Settle committed motion back toward readiness with hips/shoulders visibly distinct from impact and idle; maintain hands/equipment and target-facing. No body mirroring.

## hurt

Clear recoil/compression away from damage while eyes/head retain active-target orientation, hands/feet/tail intact. No flash/trail painted into body; no attack pose renamed.

## defeat

Distinct defeated/downed posture with complete canonical silhouette, natural supporting/ground anatomy and retained equipment contact where applicable. No standing idle or body clipping.

## victory_return

Distinct modest confident release-to-ready gesture; canonical personality, relaxed body weight, complete equipment/feet/tail. No new outfit, trophy or celebration FX baked.

## rest

Same NPC in relaxed stationary full-body pose; clear face and hands, complete feet/hem at baseline.

## route/walk

Same NPC in actual route/walk step, carrying only canonical activity props; contact foot at baseline, lifted foot complete.

## work/read/tend

Canonical NPC-specific workstation activity: Patch works at existing forge/workbench; Ilex reads/writes established records; Sera tends/inspects her existing role. Hands visibly contact only established props. Do not invent a new profession or tool.

## inspect

Same NPC visibly examining established prop/station; head and relevant hand contacts readable; not neutral relabelled.

## talk

Same NPC conversational gesture with readable face, stable full body and canonical props; keep dialogue UI separate.

## sit

Same NPC seated with complete hips/legs/feet and chair/contact documented; character only, furniture separate from body unless exact intrinsic prop restriction declared.

## offer

Merchant deliberately presents existing stock/offer with visible supporting hands, readable face and full body. No new items/price text painted in.

## return

Merchant returns from offer/inspection to ready, distinct weight/gesture and same source identity.

## phase transition

Distinct boss phase-changing posture from existing mechanics, full same body; no new identity/anatomy/equipment inferred. Transient transformation effects separate.

## vulnerability

Readable exposed/resting boss opening from current mechanics; same anatomy, distinct weight/limb lowering; no visual suggestion of an invented mechanic.
