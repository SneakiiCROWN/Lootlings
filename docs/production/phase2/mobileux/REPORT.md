## Delivered behavior

- A shared touch gesture owner distinguishes taps from drags at 12 logical pixels. Vertical content can be dragged over list cards or empty list space, with bounded decaying inertia. Rich-text details also scroll by finger. Scrollbars are position indicators, and settings sliders retain native drag handling. Single primary finger, canceled touches, state changes and synthetic mouse duplication are guarded.
- Combat touches start inside the arena, outside interactive controls, and attack on deliberate release. HUD, menus, confirmation overlays, swipes, travel and cinematics cannot become touch attacks. Existing immediate attacks, automatic attacks and 110 ms combat tap cooldown remain. A new press starts a new gesture, so the post-drag mouse guard does not swallow the next intentional tap.
- Upgrade, expedition merchant and boss reward cards inspect before confirming. Confirm has a 180 ms spillover guard; costs, item identity and irreversible alternatives are visible. Rerolls, caches, permanent purchases and salvage show their consequences. Hideout stock retains its existing offer selection plus separate BUY, and stash equipment retains inspection plus EQUIP. Ordinary reversible navigation does not require confirmation. Equipping into a full stash now explicitly warns before destroying the old non-starter item. Existing mechanic validation, prices and economy are preserved.
- Android Back and desktop Escape dismiss confirmation first, return submenus to the hub, pause combat, or resume from Pause. Required reward decisions remain required. Hub actions are shorter and spatially recognizable: BEGIN EXPEDITION, UPGRADES, SHOP, GEAR and ARCHIVE. Home shows currencies and current weapon; full equipment and power details live in their destination screens. Smaller hideout/settings buttons have at least 48 logical pixel target height. Selected stash filters remain disabled/marked; shop offer selection remains highlighted.
- Phone/tablet presentation uses the existing uniformly scaled, centered 480x854 portrait composition inside safe bounds. Fourteen size/cutout cases cover 360x640 through 1200x1920. Wider tablets retain a bounded portrait column, rather than stretching lists across the full width. Letterbox background now matches the navy branding. Landscape and independently rearranged tablet columns are not delivered.
- Canonical Relic Beast art supplies a text-free legacy icon, adaptive foreground/background and monochrome layer. Existing wordmark supplies desktop boot branding; Android system splash uses the canonical foreground while redundant engine splash is disabled. No new generated final art. [Actual APK resources](actual-apk-branding.png) were decoded and compared with source pixels, including system splash; the splash differs from the template's default. [Godot 4.7 options](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_editorexportplatformandroid.html#class-editorexportplatformandroid-property-splash-screen-icon).

## Smallest physical-device acceptance

1. Transfer the replacement APK to the S23 and open it in My Files to **update the existing app**. Keep the installed app/data; do not uninstall. Check the home-screen icon, then cold-launch: canonical icon/branding, readable first menu, no robot flash or excessive delay.
2. Play for 10-15 minutes. Swipe Stash/Archive/campaign/details over both cards and empty space; try a drag that ends over a button, then a deliberate tap. Tap HUD, Pause/Resume, shop/upgrade/reward cards: no extra attack, no charge/choice before the intended confirmation, no repeated charge. Try Back and a canceled confirmation. Inspect gear/equip/salvage, text/cutout edges and reward cards. Compare melee/ranged/magic if gear permits; listen for weight, distinct families and fatigue at normal speaker/headphone volume. Watch VFX and crowded combat readability, hitching or rising heat.
3. Background during combat, return and explicitly Resume; finish a decision/run, then relaunch and verify committed gear/currency/progress. Open **Pause > TEST REPORT** after sustained combat and send the visible frame/loading results plus any issue and its screen/weapon. For memory/log evidence, connect USB with debugging approved and run `python tools/collect_android.py`; it only reads device diagnostics. If a tablet is available, repeat the scrolling/reading portion there in portrait.

Known limits: live expeditions do not survive Android process death; committed progress persists. Physical touch feel, cold-launch time, sustained FPS/frame pacing, GPU behavior, Android PSS/thermal trend, system safe-area treatment and subjective sound acceptance remain unmeasured for this APK. Android release signing/template and store readiness are separate. Next production work should respond to measured device feedback, then address canonical fallback anatomy, boss animation and final music.
