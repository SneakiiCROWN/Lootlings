# Heavy stance pack — authored beta provenance and acceptance

Built-in image generation/editing was used with the five existing adult runtime bodies as identity-preserving edit targets and the existing heavy weapons as design references. Originals remain unchanged. No external/licensed asset was introduced.

## Deliverables

Ten bodies: `art/sprites/poses/{vault_runner,ruin_warden,nightstage_drifter,velvet_astrologer,ashtrail_hunter}/heavy_two_hand_{ready,release}.png` (1024×1536 RGBA).
Three separate held exports: `art/sprites/weapons/stances/{greatsword,greataxe,maul}_held.png` (512×512 RGBA). Their original generated paintings are preserved in `art/source/held_heavy_v1/`; `tools/export_authored_held.gd` only resizes them.

`art/production_pose_manifest.json` is authoritative: 216 explicit actor/group/phase rows, ten authored-beta bodies, measured normalized hand/Core anchors, registered rectangles/feet baseline, separate held overrides and draw depth. ATTACK/IMPACT use RELEASE; other phases retain READY plus the existing lightweight action transforms. No new attack/damage/timing rules.

All five adults × greatsword/greataxe/maul × READY/RELEASE = **30 right-facing states** rendered and visually inspected at native OpenGL 480×854. Distinct silhouettes/materials, natural bent arms, real two-hand shaft contact, loaded stance and planted release remain visible. Held art draws behind the painted hands. The Astrologer's long braid overlaps the carried weapon; her face remains unobscured. No fake limbs or detached support hand layers.

This is a beta reusable-pose pack, not skeletal animation. The READY→RELEASE art change follows the existing short action phase; no in-between drawings. Asymmetric opposite-facing exports are **not** completed: their explicit flag uses the prior neutral fallback instead of mirroring the new poses. This reduces the original 108 support-hand-incomplete right-facing combinations to **93**, not zero. Creatures, ranged/polearm/caster/dual groups and actual paired weapon exports remain required. Gloomlet is untouched/selectable. All twelve families retain gameplay.

## Production and QA notes

A Warden candidate added an incorrect second shoulder plate; it was rejected and corrected to the original single image-right pauldron. A sword candidate had a decorative middle handle collar; it was corrected to an uninterrupted grip. Rejected candidates were not copied into the repository. Generated source files remain in the tool's original output directory.

Native capture command (isolated APPDATA recommended):
```powershell
& C:\Godot\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --path . --rendering-method gl_compatibility --resolution 480x854 --script tests/authored_pose_qa.gd -- --visual
```
Captures go to ignored `.godot_test_data/`. Five sheets arrange greatsword / greataxe / maul left-to-right and READY / RELEASE top-to-bottom. Also inspect the full-resolution files. Assertions cover actual image dimensions/alpha/anchor opacity, 216 unique manifest keys, two-hand contact, depth, and explicit unsupported opposite-facing state. Existing 144-combination test still distinguishes missing art from mathematical passes.

Memory: only one active authored body pair is retained by the resolver (about 12 MiB RGBA before driver/compression overhead); three 512² held exports add about 3 MiB. Missing groups are negatively cached. No new continuous processing, nodes, shaders, particles or gameplay RNG calls. Desktop native QA is not low-end Android certification. Additional body art increases package size.
